      How a match works

      Nobody is driving. Each fighter is a ball bouncing inside the arena with a weapon on a chain — the weapon is a free point mass held at a fixed distance and driven tangentially, so it whips and lags rather than being pinned to a rotating transform. When a weapon head overlaps an enemy ball it lands a hit, capped to one hit per target per swing. Damage runs through a matchup multiplier, the attacker's passive, and the defender's armour, then the attacking element's onHit applies its signature effect. Ultimate meters fill from hits and from time, and fire the instant they cap. The whole simulation is a fixed 120 Hz and draws every random number from a seeded generator, which is why a seed replays exactly.
