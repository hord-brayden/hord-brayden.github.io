Innovation Highlights

Agentic AI ad-buying agent. LLM-driven media buyer on Claude via AWS Bedrock with 36 custom platform tools. Takes raw emails and PM input and executes buys across DSPs and social platforms with full PM-system traceability.
Pixel Lab. Chrome extension for safely testing ad pixels and proving consent compliance: live view of every platform collecting data, consent simulation with plain-English proof of what each platform learned, ad-click-to-purchase journey checks, and a Safe mode that blocks delivery at the browser level. In private beta.
Bid Inspector. Chrome extension that shows the header-bidding auction as it happens in the browser: every Prebid, Amazon TAM, and Google Ad Manager bid including the ones that lost, the creatives and advertisers behind them, decoded identifiers, consent state, and the targeting sent. Includes a targeting injector with per-rule verification, session snapshots with compare, and redacted HTML and Excel reports. In private beta.
Creative Finder. Semantic search across a library of thousands of mail pieces and digital ads. Plain-English queries ranked by a CLIP model running locally, so no creative leaves the network. Facet filters, PDF rendering, ZIP and slide-deck export, usage monitoring. Flask and SQLite, deployable on a LAN Mac or a $22/month cloud box.
Datum Veil. Privacy-preserving Python tool that lets proprietary datasets be used with third-party AI systems without handing the data over: records are pseudonymized, totals stay exact, and the guarantees are documented in plain language for leadership and client security questionnaires.
AONS, the Ad Object Naming System. Naming system for ad objects, deployed across StackAdapt, Meta, LinkedIn, TikTok, and Google, designed so reports join cleanly across platforms instead of being reconciled by hand.
Generative-Engine Optimization (GEO). Designed and shipped agentic-search optimization for the organic marketing pipeline and automated RFP responses, capturing government contract opportunities others were missing.
Direct-Mail Innovation Research. Led technical research and integration design for physical/digital attribution. Evaluated USPS Informed Visibility, PURL systems, BLE beacons, conductive ink, and Digimarc as a portfolio approach to bridging postal infrastructure with downstream digital signal.
Eight-platform abstraction layer. Standardized CS-to-Media handoffs across Meta, Programmatic, YouTube, Search, Native, X, LinkedIn, and Reddit into a single normalized schema and versioned spec, recovering 62 weekly team hours.
Build-vs-buy at scale. Replaced a $50K/yr LiveRamp vendor feature with an Apps Script pipeline that parses 12-13 daily emails, dedupes CSVs, and refreshes every 15 minutes. Same outcome, zero recurring cost.
Marketing Mix Modeling. Google Meridian and Facebook Robyn alongside FFT and Bayesian causal-inference techniques to produce defensible attribution from messy performance data.
Edge-compute AI. CLIP-based image classification and Ollama thin-model deployments on Arduino NICLA and Raspberry Pi, making inference local, cheap, and fast.
Production Claude integration. Arena Regulatus Slack bot deployed via Make.com to the Claude API to Slack, tuned to enforce QA culture and add personality to the media-ops workflow.
Cryptographic campaign-ID hashing. MD5 aggregate key that solved a chronic cross-system join problem (typos and freehand campaign names). Running in production for years and trusted by data, sales, and PM teams alike.
